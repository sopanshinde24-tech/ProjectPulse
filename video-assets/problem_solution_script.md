# Video A — Problem and Solution Script (45–60 Seconds)

**Title:** From Scattered Spreadsheets to Evidence-Based Portfolio Intelligence  
**Target Duration:** 55 Seconds  
**Pacing:** 145 words per minute (Crisp, executive documentary tone)  
**Disclaimer:** All records displayed on screen are labeled *Synthetic Demonstration Data*.

---

## Segment 1: 00:00 – 00:10 | Scattered Tracking & Poor Visibility
- **Visual Direction:** Open on a split-screen comparison showing fragmented departmental trackers—disconnected spreadsheets, stale slide decks, and email threads across Technology, Finance, Operations, and Marketing—before transitioning into the clean ProjectPulse AI header.
- **On-Screen Callout:** `12 Strategic Initiatives · 4 Departments · Disconnected Status Reporting`
- **Narrator (Voiceover):**  
  "When an organization manages dozens of strategic initiatives across Technology, Finance, Operations, and Marketing, critical project data gets trapped in disconnected spreadsheets and static weekly slide decks."

## Segment 2: 00:10 – 00:20 | How Delays, Risks & Budget Overruns Affect Decisions
- **Visual Direction:** Zoom into red variance indicators: a core ledger migration running $190,000 over budget, an overdue security milestone, and an unmitigated vendor risk that wasn't escalated in time.
- **On-Screen Callout:** `Unspotted Schedule Slippage + Unchecked Budget Variance = Delayed Executive Action`
- **Narrator (Voiceover):**  
  "Without a single source of truth, overdue milestones, budget overruns, and compounding technical risks stay hidden until deadlines are already missed—forcing leadership to react too late."

## Segment 3: 00:20 – 00:45 | Introducing ProjectPulse AI (Portfolio, Milestones, Risk & Budget)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **00:20–00:27:** Reveal the **ProjectPulse AI Executive Dashboard** in crisp enterprise paper-and-navy styling. Cursor highlights live calculated KPIs ($14.55M allocated budget, $13.84M actual spend, 95.1% utilization, and Green/Amber/Red health distribution).
  - **00:27–00:33:** Switch to **Project Portfolio & Milestone Tracking**, filtering down to Delayed projects and inspecting overdue completion dates alongside timestamped progress history.
  - **00:33–00:39:** Switch to the **Risk Register**, sorting by Quantitative Risk Score (`Probability × Impact = 20/25`) and updating a mitigation progress bar.
  - **00:39–00:45:** Switch to **Budget Monitoring**, revealing department-level variance rollups and identifying the 3 initiatives exceeding 100% budget utilization.
- **On-Screen Callout:** `Live Relational KPIs · Quantitative Risk Scoring · Departmental Variance Rollups`
- **Narrator (Voiceover):**  
  "ProjectPulse AI centralizes your entire project portfolio into one live intelligence workspace. Every KPI, health status, and departmental rollup updates automatically from underlying records. Track planned versus actual milestone dates, score critical risks consistently on a probability-by-impact matrix, and pinpoint budget variances down to the exact dollar."

## Segment 4: 00:45 – 00:55 | Evidence-Based AI Executive Report
- **Visual Direction:** Open the **AI Executive Assistant** module. Click **Generate AI Executive Brief**. Highlight how each recommended follow-up action displays explicit supporting evidence badges (`PRJ-102 · +$190k Overrun`, `RSK-201 · Score 20/25`), followed by the human review approval toggle and CSV/PDF export buttons.
- **On-Screen Callout:** `Evidence-Grounded Gemini AI · Zero Hallucinated Figures · Human-in-the-Loop Approval`
- **Narrator (Voiceover):**  
  "And when it's time to brief leadership, the Gemini-powered AI Executive Assistant synthesizes your live database into an evidence-backed management report—citing exact project variances, overdue milestones, and risk scores for human review before export."
